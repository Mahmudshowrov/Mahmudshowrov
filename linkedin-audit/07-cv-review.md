## Personal data: depends on where you send it

- **For Bangladesh local employers:** father's/mother's names, DOB, marital status and permanent address are still common. You can keep them, though many modern employers no longer expect them.
- **For international employers or brand buying offices:** **remove** father's/mother's names, DOB, marital status, gender and permanent address. In the EU and US this information can work against you, and recruiters there don't expect it.
- **Referees:** you're sharing their mobile numbers and email on every copy you send out. Use **"References available on request"** instead, and give the details only when an employer is close to an offer. Also add Suneeta Wahal's company name; right now only the title is shown.
- **Don't post the CV publicly** (e.g. as a LinkedIn Featured PDF) while it holds your phone number, home address and referees' contacts.
