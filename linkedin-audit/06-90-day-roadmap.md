# 6. 90-Day Growth Roadmap

## Before you start: record your baseline (Day 1)

Write these down in the tracker. Without a baseline, the KPIs mean nothing.
- Connections, followers
- Profile views (last 90 days), search appearances (last 7 days). Both are on your profile dashboard.
- Number of recommendations, number of skills with endorsements
- Number of posts in the last 90 days

## Days 1–7: profile optimisation

| Day | Task | Time |
|----:|------|-----:|
| 1 | Record the baseline. Turn **off** "Share profile updates with your network" (Settings → Visibility) so the edits don't spam people. | 20 min |
| 1 | Send me the profile PDF/screenshots so I can finalise the audit and score | 5 min |
| 2 | Gather your real numbers (the questions in the README) | 1–2 hrs |
| 3 | Update the headline, About, and Top 5 skills | 45 min |
| 4 | Rewrite all 4 experience entries; tag skills on each role; link each entry to its company page | 1.5 hrs |
| 5 | Banner (Canva) + photo + contact info + "Open to" settings | 1.5 hrs |
| 6 | Add all 30 skills; remove irrelevant ones; ask 10 colleagues for endorsements | 45 min |
| 7 | Build Featured (capability one-pager + costing sample); send 3 recommendation requests with drafts. Turn profile-update sharing back **on**. | 2 hrs |

**KPIs by Day 7:** profile 100% complete · 3 recommendation requests sent · search appearances higher than the baseline.

## Days 8–30: personal branding and networking

- **Post:** weeks 2–4 of the calendar (3 posts a week, 9–10 total).
- **Comment:** 5 thoughtful comments a day on posts by target people (3–4 sentences that add a point, not "Great post!").
- **Connect:** about 40 targeted requests a week (see the target table).
- **Learn:** start an Excel/Power Query project you can post about (e.g. the T&A tracker), which feeds Post #10.

**KPIs by Day 30:**
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| New relevant connections (brand/sourcing/recruiter/factory) | 120–150 |
| Connection acceptance rate | ≥ 50% |
| Profile views (30 days) | 2× the baseline |
| Recommendations received | ≥ 2 |
| Avg. comments per post (from industry people) | ≥ 5 |

## Days 31–60: authority building and engagement

- **Post:** weeks 5–8, adding the first carousel (costing breakdown).
- **Engage deeper:** start 2–3 real conversations a week over DM with people who engaged with your posts. Give value first (answer a question, share a template).
- **Collaborate:** comment on, and tag in, posts by 2–3 people from your benchmarking list. Suggest swapping insights.
- **Featured:** add your best-performing post.
- **Recruiters:** reach out to 10 apparel recruiters with template C, plus a follow-up.

**KPIs by Day 60:**
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Total new relevant connections since Day 1 | 300+ |
| Followers growth | +25–40% vs baseline |
| Meaningful conversations (DM threads with 3+ replies on industry topics) | 10+ |
| Posts with 10+ comments | 2+ |
| Recruiter conversations | 3+ |
| Recommendations | ≥ 3 total |

## Days 61–90: business development and career opportunities

- **Post:** weeks 9–13, including the soft introduction to the buying-house idea (week 13) without naming NextGen yet.
- **Career:** apply to 5–10 targeted roles (sourcing offices, PD manager roles), each with a personalised message to the hiring manager or recruiter.
- **Business groundwork:** have 5 informational chats with buying house owners or brand sourcing people about what brands actually need from a buying partner. Take notes; this is free market research for NextGen.
- **Review:** look at the tracker. Which pillar and format worked best? Double down on it in the next 90 days.

**KPIs by Day 90:**
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Total new relevant connections since Day 1 | 450–500 |
| Profile views (90 days) | 3× the baseline |
| Inbound recruiter/job messages | 3+ |
| Interviews | 1–3 |
| Informational chats with buying house owners / brand sourcing people | 5+ |
| Qualified business leads (a brand-side person who says they'd consider a sourcing partner like you in the future) | 2–3 |
| Posting consistency | ≥ 33 of 39 planned posts |

**About the targets:** these are realistic ranges for a niche B2B profile that starts with a few hundred to a few thousand connections. They aren't guarantees. If your baseline is very different, tell me and I'll recalibrate. What really counts is the **quality of conversations and opportunities**, not likes.

## Weekly routine (about 4 hours a week)

| Day | Task | Time |
|-----|------|-----:|
| Mon | Draft 3 posts for the week; send 15 connection requests | 60 min |
| Tue–Thu | Post at the planned time; reply to every comment within 2 hrs; 5 comments on others' posts; 8 requests | 30 min/day |
| Fri | Follow-ups and DMs; 5 requests | 30 min |
| Sat/Sun | Update the tracker (10 min); learn 1 Excel/Power BI skill to post about | 40 min |
