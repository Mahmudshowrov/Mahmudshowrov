# 5. Reels & Video Strategy + 10 Ready-to-Shoot Scripts

## 5.1 Principles

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **First 3 seconds** | Start with motion plus a bold statement or question on screen. No logo intro, no "Assalamu alaikum, welcome to our page". |
| **Silent-first** | Most people watch muted. On-screen text has to carry the message on its own. Use Bangla in bold sans, max 6 words per line. |
| **Show, don't claim** | Every claim (strong, spacious, real leather) gets a visual test. |
| **Price at the end** | Show the price in the last 2–3 seconds, so viewers who stay are interested buyers. |
| **Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920** | Shoot in 4K/60fps if the phone allows. Exports at 1080p look sharper. |
| **Light** | Window light (side) plus a white foam board bounce. Avoid ceiling tube lights. |
| **Consistency** | Same font, same colour grade, same end card (logo + "Send Message"). |
| **Music** | Use Meta's licensed sound library for ads. Trending audio is fine for organic posts, but copyrighted music can block boosting. |

**Kit (~৳6–10k):** phone tripod with remote, 2 LED softbox or ring lights, white and beige backdrop paper, a clip-on mic for testimonials, and a small turntable for 360° shots.

**Editing:** CapCut (free). Use a template: white text, black 40% shadow, cut every 1–1.5 seconds.

### Script 1 — "Laptop, but make it handbag" (Everyday Tote)
**Duration:** 20s · **Audience:** working women

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Voiceover (optional, Bangla) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Close-up: a 14" laptop slides into the tote | **"Laptop dhoray?"** | "Laptop dhoray?" |
| 3–6s | Bag zipped, lifted. Clean silhouette, no bulge | **"Kintu dekhte handbag"** | "Kintu dekhte ekdom handbag." |
| 6–11s | Inside tour: sleeve, zip pocket, pouch | "Laptop sleeve · Zip pocket · Pouch" | — |
| 11–16s | Model walks out of an office, bag on shoulder | "Office → Café → Home" | "Shara din, ek bag." |
| 16–20s | Product on beige, price + logo end card | "Everyday Tote · ৳[price] · COD" | "Order korte Send Message." |

**Music/editing:** soft lo-fi or minimal house, cut on the beat, warm grade.
**Caption:** "Laptop + lunch + lipstick, all in one 👜 The Everyday Tote. 14" laptop sleeve, zip pocket, detachable pouch. ৳[price] · COD all over BD · 7-day exchange. Tap Send Message to order."

## 5.3 Production workflow

1. **Batch:** shoot 4–5 scripts on one Saturday.
2. **Make 3 hooks per video** (different first 3 seconds, same body). Use them to A/B test ads.
3. **Keep raw clips** in a Google Drive folder per product. They become inbox reply videos.
4. **Measure:** 3-second view rate (hook), ThruPlay rate (body), and **messages started** (sale).
