## A. Brand identity

| # | What to check | Common problem (*hypothesis, verify*) | Business impact | Recommended fix | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Page name / username | The name "BAG LUX" is generic and has a namesake abroad. The username `baglux.me` reads like a domain. | Hard to search and easy to confuse with others. "Lux" also suggests luxury, which raises the bar for proof. | Keep BAG LUX, but register the same handle everywhere: `baglux.bd` or `bagluxbd` on Instagram, TikTok and a domain. Use the tagline **"BAG LUX — Premium Bags, Bangladesh"** in the page name or intro. | High |
| A2 | Profile picture | A detailed logo that becomes unreadable at small size, or a product photo used as the logo. | The logo is what people see in comments, ads and Messenger. If it's weak, the page looks like a reseller. | A simple wordmark or monogram ("BL"), one colour on a solid background, readable at 40px. | High |
| A3 | Cover photo | A collage of many bags, sale text, or Canva templates. | Collages say "wholesale market". Premium brands show one hero product. | One hero bag, styled, with soft shadow and lots of empty space. Add one line: "Crafted for everyday luxury". Use a 1640×624 design with the important part kept inside the mobile-safe centre. Change it monthly with the collection. | High |
| A4 | Intro / About | Missing or vague text. No delivery, payment or return policy. | Customers ask the same five questions in the inbox, which slows conversion and loses impatient buyers. | Use the 101-character intro: "Premium bags for men & women 🇧🇩 COD all over BD · 7-day exchange · Inbox to order". In About, add policies, business hours, WhatsApp and website. | High |
| A5 | CTA button | "Send message" is fine, but WhatsApp is often not linked. | Some buyers prefer WhatsApp. If it's not linked, they get one more step and may leave. | Set the CTA to **Send Message**. Link WhatsApp Business. Turn on Messenger **instant reply** and **FAQs**. | High |
| A6 | Visual consistency | Each post uses a different background, filter and font. | No recognisable brand. Thumbnails in the feed don't build memory. | Lock a brand kit: 2 colours, 1 font and 1 background family. See `03-brand-and-product.md`. | Medium |
| A7 | Established brand vs reseller | Supplier photos, watermarks from other sellers, "copy" of designer brands. | **This is the biggest premium killer.** Customers who reverse-search an image and find it on AliExpress or another BD page will stop trusting you. Fake designer logos also risk Meta ad rejection and IP takedowns. | Shoot **all** product photos yourself. Never use designer names or logos ("LV style", "Gucci inspired") in captions or ads. | High |

## B. Content audit

| # | What to check | Common problem (*hypothesis*) | Impact | Fix | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Product photography | Phone photos on a bed or table, with mixed lighting. | The bag looks cheaper than it is, which makes it hard to charge premium prices. | Build a ৳3–5k home studio: white sweep, 2 softbox lights, tripod. Shoot every bag 7 ways (see `03`). | High |
| B2 | Video / Reels | Few reels, or slideshows of still photos. | Reels are where new, non-follower reach comes from on Facebook now. Without them, organic reach depends on ads. | 3–4 reels a week, using the scripts in `05-reels-scripts.md`. | High |
| B3 | Captions | "Price inbox", "DM for price", no product details. | **"Price inbox" filters out serious buyers and attracts time-wasters.** Many customers scroll past rather than ask. | Always show the price. State size, material, compartments and colours. Add a clear CTA. | High |

| B5 | Repetition | The same angle, layout and "available" caption on every post. | People stop noticing, and Facebook shows the posts to fewer people. | Rotate 5 content pillars (see `04`). | Medium |
| B6 | Best performers | *Unknown without Insights.* | — | Export Insights. Rank posts by **messages started** and **link clicks**, not likes. | High |

## C. Customer engagement

| # | Check | Common problem (*hypothesis*) | Impact | Fix | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Comment replies | "Inbox please" replies to every comment. | Other readers see no answer and assume the price is high or hidden. | Reply with the price and one benefit in public, then move to inbox: "৳3,450 apu, real leather lining 😊 Inbox e details pathacchi". | High |
| C2 | Response time | Replies come hours later, especially at night (9pm–1am is peak browsing). | Hot leads go cold. They message 3 pages and buy from whoever replies first. | Set up an instant reply, saved replies (see `07`), and a 10am–1am rota. Target median under 10 min. | High |
| C3 | Reviews / recommendations | Reviews tab off, or few reviews. | No social proof means a high-ticket COD purchase feels risky. | Turn on Reviews. After delivery, run a review-request flow (see `07`). Post customer photos weekly. | High |
| C4 | Customer photos / UGC | None. | Weak trust. | Put a review card in every parcel with a small reward for a photo review. | Medium |
| C5 | Trust issues to look for in comments | "Real leather?", "Same as picture?", "Fake?" | These are objections. Every one left unanswered is lost sales. | Answer them with content: material test reels, "photo vs delivered" posts, and an exchange policy. | High |

## D. Sales conversion

**Typical journey today (*hypothesis*):** sees post → comments "price?" → waits → gets price in inbox → asks delivery charge → asks real photo → silence → no order.

**Target journey:** sees reel or ad (price visible) → taps "Send message" → instant reply with price, sizes, delivery and an "Order now" button → gives name, phone and address in 1 message → confirmation within 5 min → call or WhatsApp confirm before dispatch → delivery → review request → repeat offer at day 30.

| # | Friction | Impact | Fix | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Hidden price | Lost scrollers, low-quality enquiries | Show price on every post and ad | High |
| D2 | Unclear delivery charge / time | Questions slow the sale and cause drop-offs | Fixed line in every caption: "Dhaka ৳X (1–2 days) · Outside ৳Y (2–4 days)" | High |
| D3 | Multi-step data collection | Customer gets bored | One order form message (see `07`) | High |
| D4 | Fear of fake / wrong product | No order, or refused on delivery | Real video in inbox on request, open-box check policy, exchange policy | High |
| D5 | High COD refusal / returns (RTO) | Silent margin killer: you pay courier both ways | Phone confirmation, small advance via bKash/Nagad for outside Dhaka or high-value bags, block repeat refusers | High |
| D6 | No follow-up on "I'll think" | Most enquiries lost | 3-touch follow-up (see `07`) | High |
