# 8. 30/60/90-Day Roadmap + Weekly Dashboard

KPI targets marked **[set from baseline]** must be set after the first 2 weeks of your own data. Inventing them now would be guessing. The method: take your week-2 baseline and aim for the improvement shown.

**KPIs (Day 30):**
- Posting consistency: 30/30 posts done.
- Median first-reply time: under 10 min.
- Price visible on 100% of product posts.
- Baseline recorded: cost per message, message → delivered order rate, RTO %, cost per delivered order.
- Reviews: first 10+ genuine reviews.

## Weekly dashboard

A ready-made sheet is in `BAG_LUX_Pricing_and_Dashboard.xlsx` (tab **Weekly Dashboard**). Formulas are built in, and you enter the yellow cells only.

| Metric | Where to get it | Formula | Use it to decide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total page reach | Business Suite → Insights | — | Is content reaching new people? |
| Post engagement | Insights | reactions + comments + shares | Which topics work (secondary metric) |
| Reel views | Insights → Reels | — | Hook quality (check 3-sec views) |
| Profile visits | Insights | — | Is the page convincing visitors? |
| Messenger enquiries | Inbox / Ads Manager "messaging conversations started" | — | Demand volume |
| WhatsApp enquiries | WhatsApp Business count | — | Channel mix |
| Total orders | Order log | — | — |
| Confirmed orders | Order log (after phone check) | — | Quality of leads |
| Delivered orders | Courier report | — | **Real sales** |
| Cancelled / returned orders | Courier report | — | RTO problem? |
| Total sales revenue | Delivered orders × price | Σ delivered order value | — |
| Advertising expenditure | Ads Manager | — | — |
| Cost per enquiry | | Ad spend ÷ (Messenger + WhatsApp enquiries) | Ad efficiency |
| Conversion rate | | Delivered orders ÷ total enquiries | Inbox / sales skill |
| Customer acquisition cost (CAC) | | Ad spend ÷ delivered orders (new customers) | **The number that matters for scaling** |
| RTO % | | Returned ÷ dispatched | Logistics and lead quality |
| Gross profit | | Revenue − COGS − fulfilment (incl. RTO losses) | Product/price health |
| Net profit | | Gross profit − ad spend − other marketing − fixed costs share | **Is the business making money?** |

**Weekly decision rules:**
1. **CAC ≤ target and stock is fine →** raise the winning ad budget 20–30%.
2. **Cost per enquiry is fine but conversion is low →** the problem is the inbox (speed, scripts, price shock), not the ads.
3. **Conversion is fine but RTO is high →** tighten confirmation and advance payment, and cut bad areas.
4. **Cost per enquiry is rising and frequency is up →** creative fatigue. Launch new hooks.
5. **Gross margin is low →** fix the price or the cost (renegotiate supplier, packaging, courier rate). Don't spend more on ads.
