## What the evidence shows

### The market has three clear tiers, with a weak middle

| Tier | Price band (evidence-based) | Who's there | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mass / marketplace | ৳400–1,800 | Bikroy, FB Marketplace, most FB pages | Fake brands, poor photos, no trust |
| Mid fashion (mostly PU) | ৳2,000–3,000 | KalonBD, BagBaba, Patchee | Generic designs, "luxury" used loosely |
| Craft leather | ৳2,300–10,000 | Gootipa, Banah, LeatherDot, La Alba, HMS, Jawda | Strong product. Often traditional, heritage-led look. Weaker reels and Facebook selling. |

### What competitors do better (likely, verify on pages)

1. **Physical proof:** a showroom (Gootipa, Bags N Packs), press coverage (Gootipa, Banah, LeatherDot) and a website. These make a brand look real.
2. **Warranty as a weapon:** LeatherDot and Ventro use a 3-year warranty. In a market full of fakes, a warranty tells the buyer "we'll still be here".
3. **Origin story:** "Made in Bangladesh" and "local artisans" give a reason to pay more that isn't just a logo.
4. **Reviews:** Bags N Packs shows 27 reviews with 88% recommending. Even a small count beats zero.

### What customers respond to (inferred, check in Ad Library)

- A visible price plus a clear material claim ("genuine leather", "YKK zip").
- Problem-solving bags: laptop fit, office-to-evening, travel.
- Reassurance: COD, exchange, warranty, a real address.

### Market gaps BAG LUX can exploit

1. **"Modern premium" between PU fashion and heritage leather.** Most leather brands look traditional, and most fashion brands look cheap. A clean, minimal, *Instagram-native* brand at ৳2,500–6,500 is under-served.
2. **Reels-first selling.** Craft brands sell through websites and press, not short video. BAG LUX can beat them on Facebook with better video.
3. **Men's premium beyond the briefcase.** Most men's options are office bags and wallets. Slim crossbodies, sling bags and weekender totes for young professionals are an open space.
4. **Transparent quality proof.** Almost nobody shows stitch counts, hardware weight, scratch tests and "photo vs delivered" side by side. That's how you earn "Lux" without a famous name.
5. **Service as the brand:** a 7-day exchange, 1-year hardware warranty, free repair of stitching, and a gift box as standard.

### How BAG LUX differentiates

> **"Modern premium bags, proven on camera."**
> Original designs, honest material labels, quality proof in every post, and after-sales service that makes buying on COD from a Facebook page feel safe.
