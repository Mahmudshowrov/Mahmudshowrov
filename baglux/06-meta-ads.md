# 6. Facebook & Instagram Ads Strategy (Meta Ads Manager)

## 6.1 Ground rules

- **No guaranteed results and no borrowed benchmarks.** Your targets come from **your margin** (see `03-brand-and-product.md` §3.7): *Target cost per message = Target CAC × enquiry-to-delivered rate.*
- **Use Ads Manager, not the "Boost" button.** Boosting gives you less control over objective, placements and testing.
- **Measure the real outcome: delivered orders.** Messages that never convert, and COD refusals, both cost money. Match every ad's messages to your order log weekly.
- **Ad policy:** never use designer names or logos, "replica", or before/after body claims. Ad accounts in Bangladesh get restricted easily, and a banned account stops all sales.

## 6.2 Simple definitions

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **Campaign objective** | What you tell Meta to find: "people who will message" (Engagement → Messages) or "people who will buy on a website" (Sales). |
| **Ad set** | The audience + budget + placement group |
| **CPM** | Cost to show your ad 1,000 times |
| **CTR** | % of people who saw the ad and clicked |
| **Cost per message** | Ad spend ÷ new conversations started |
| **Retargeting** | Showing ads to people who already watched, engaged or messaged |
| **Advantage+ audience** | Meta's automatic targeting. Often works well once you give it strong creatives. |
| **Learning phase** | The first days after launch or after big edits, when results swing. Don't judge too early, and don't edit every day. |
| **Frequency** | Average times one person saw your ad. Rising frequency with falling results = creative fatigue. |

## 6.3 Audience building blocks

| Audience | Setup |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Start with Dhaka city + Chattogram + Sylhet city (radius), where courier delivery is fastest and returns are usually lower. Test a "Rest of Bangladesh" ad set separately, so you can compare cost **and** RTO rate. |
| **Age / gender** | Women's line: women 22–40. Men's line: men 24–45. Gifting ads: both, 24–45. |
| **Interests (broad starting point)** | Handbags, Fashion accessories, Online shopping, Business travel, Office/professional interests. Use these only for initial tests, then let Advantage+ go broad. |
| **Warm (retargeting)** | Video viewers 50%+ (30 days) · Page/IG engagers (60 days) · People who messaged but didn't order (30 days) |
| **Customers** | Upload the delivered-customer phone list (with consent, per privacy rules) → exclude them from cold ads, and use for repeat/referral ads → Lookalike 1–3% once you have a few hundred customers |
| **Exclusions** | Exclude past buyers from cold acquisition. Exclude people who refused COD (keep your own blacklist). |

## 6.4 Campaign types

| Campaign | Objective | Destination | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Message campaign** (main seller) | Engagement → Messages / maximise conversations | Messenger + WhatsApp | From day 1 |
| **B. Video views** (cheap warm-audience builder) | Engagement → ThruPlay | — | Starter optional, Growth+ |
| **C. Retargeting** | Messages | Messenger / WhatsApp | Once warm audiences reach a usable size (Meta shows the estimate) |
| **D. Sales conversion** | Sales (website + Pixel/CAPI) | Website / landing page | Only after a website with Pixel tracking and a checkout exists (Days 61–90) |

## 6.5 Three budget scenarios

### Starter — BDT 300/day (~৳9,000/month)

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Structure | **1 campaign** (Messages) → **1 ad set** (Dhaka + Chattogram, broad/Advantage+, age per product line) → **3 ads** |
| Allocation | 100% to the Messages campaign. Don't split ৳300 into many ad sets, because each would get too little data. |
| Creatives | 3 creatives for **one** hero product: (1) a reel, Script 1 or 4; (2) the 7-shot carousel; (3) a "photo vs delivered" trust video |
| Testing period | Run 5–7 days without editing. Then switch off the weakest ad and add a new hook. |
| KPIs | Cost per message, message → order rate, cost per delivered order, RTO % |
| Scale up | When cost per delivered order stays under your Target CAC for 7 days → move to Growth |
| Cut back | Cost per delivered order is above break-even CAC after 7 days → change the **creative** first, then the product. Don't keep raising the budget on a loser. |

### Growth — BDT 700/day (~৳21,000/month)

| Campaign | Daily | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| A. Messages — Women's hero | ৳300 | Best tote/crossbody creatives |
| A. Messages — Men's hero | ৳250 | Briefcase / sling |
| C. Retargeting — Messages | ৳150 | Video viewers 50%, engagers, non-buyer messagers. Use trust content (reviews, unboxing, policies) plus an offer. |

**Creative needs:** 6–8 live ads, with 2 new hooks per week. **Test one variable at a time** (hook, product or offer).
**Testing period:** 7 days per test round.
**KPIs:** add ROAS on delivered revenue (delivered revenue ÷ ad spend), new vs returning customers, and frequency (refresh when rising and results fall).
**Increase:** raise a winning ad set's budget by about 20–30% every 3–4 days. Big jumps reset learning.
**Reduce:** pause ads with cost per delivered order above break-even for 5+ days, or with frequency rising while CTR falls.

### Scaling — BDT 1,500/day (~৳45,000/month)

| Campaign | Daily | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| A. Messages — Advantage+ broad, best 3–5 creatives | ৳700 | Main volume engine |
| A2. Messages — New creative testing | ৳250 | 3–4 new creatives per week. Promote winners into A. |
| C. Retargeting | ৳250 | Warm audiences + abandoned enquiries |
| E. Customers / Lookalike | ৳150 | Repeat purchase, referral, new collection to buyers; Lookalike 1–3% |
| B. Video views (top-of-funnel) | ৳150 | Brand reels to grow the warm pool cheaply |

**Prerequisites before scaling:** stable fulfilment (stock, packing and courier SLAs), inbox team coverage from 10am to 1am, RTO under control, and at least 2 proven hero creatives.
**Optional:** a website Sales campaign with Pixel + Conversions API once the store exists.

## 6.6 Creative testing system

```
Week 1: Test 3 HOOKS on the same video body → keep the best
Week 2: Test 3 PRODUCTS with the winning hook style → keep the best 1–2
Week 3: Test 2 OFFERS (free card holder vs free delivery) → keep the best
Week 4: Test FORMAT (reel vs carousel vs static) → set the default
```
Record every test in the dashboard's "Ad Tests" tab: date, variable, spend, messages, orders, delivered, cost per delivered order.

## 6.7 Messenger ad set-up tips

- Use a **message template** with 3 quick-reply buttons: "💰 Price & size", "🚚 Delivery info", "🛒 Order now".
- The greeting should repeat the price, so the first reply isn't wasted.
- Turn on **WhatsApp** as a destination for audiences 30+, who often prefer it. Compare conversion by channel.

## 6.8 Recommended monthly marketing budget

| Stage | Ads | Content production (props, model fees, boxes for shoots) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | ৳9,000 (Starter) | ৳5,000–10,000 | **~৳15,000–20,000** |
| Month 2 | ৳21,000 (Growth), only if Month 1 cost per delivered order ≤ target | ৳8,000–12,000 | **~৳30,000–33,000** |
| Month 3 | ৳45,000 (Scaling), only if Month 2 is profitable at Growth | ৳10,000–15,000 | **~৳55,000–60,000** |

**Rule:** the budget follows profit, not the calendar. If Month 1 doesn't hit target, stay at Starter and fix the creative, offer or price first.
