# BAG LUX — Facebook Audit & Sales Growth Strategy

**Prepared:** November 2026 · **Market:** Bangladesh · **Page:** facebook.com/baglux.me

## Bottom line

1. **The live page could not be audited directly.** Facebook was blocked from the research environment, and Insights, inbox and ad data need admin access anyway. No page statistics or score have been invented. `01-page-audit.md` lists the 6 items needed to finish the audit in 20 minutes.
2. **One verified finding:** a web search for "BAG LUX" / baglux.me returns **nothing** for your brand. The only match is an unrelated Moldovan account (@baglux.md). For a brand that claims "Lux", being invisible on Google is a trust gap.
3. **The market opportunity is clear from competitor evidence.** Bangladesh has cheap marketplace bags (৳400–1,800), generic mid-range PU fashion (৳2,000–3,000) and traditional craft-leather brands (৳2,300–10,000). Few brands are **modern, design-led and reels-first, with proof of quality on camera**. That's the BAG LUX position.
4. **The money is in conversion, not reach.** Show prices, reply in under 10 minutes, use a one-message order form, confirm by phone before dispatch, and control COD returns. Most Facebook bag sellers lose more profit to slow replies and refused parcels than to expensive ads.

## 12. Final strategic recommendations

### 1. Page score
**Not scored.** There isn't enough evidence yet. The rubric is in `01` (Brand 20 · Visuals 20 · Content 15 · Engagement 15 · Social proof 15 · Conversion 15).

### 2. Top 10 weaknesses
These are *likely, to verify*, except #1, which is verified.

1. **No search presence:** nothing appears for BAG LUX on Google. *(Verified.)*
2. A name shared with a foreign brand, and no consistent handle across platforms.
3. A risk of looking like a reseller: supplier photos, mixed visual style.
4. "Price inbox" captions that hide the price.
5. Too few reels and short videos, the main source of non-follower reach.
6. Slow or "inbox please" replies, especially at night.
7. Little social proof: reviews, customer photos, testimonials.
8. No clear delivery, exchange and warranty policy on the page.
9. Unmanaged COD refusal (RTO) cost.
10. Decisions made on likes rather than on delivered orders and CAC.

### 3. Top 10 growth opportunities
1. Own "modern premium, proven on camera" (the gap between PU fashion and heritage leather).
2. A reels-first content engine: competitors in craft leather are weaker here.
3. Men's premium beyond briefcases: slings, weekenders, pouches.
4. A warranty plus exchange guarantee (only LeatherDot and Ventro make this visible).
5. A gift-ready packaging standard, which drives gifting seasons and unboxing content.
6. Seasonal drops: Eid ×2, wedding season, Boishakh, Valentine's Day.
7. Couple and office bundles to raise the average order value.
8. A referral program that costs less than ad CAC.
9. **Corporate gifting (B2B):** your sourcing and buyer-handling experience is a real edge here.
10. A WordPress/WooCommerce or Shopify store with Pixel tracking, which unlocks Sales campaigns and Google search traffic.

### 4. Five immediate actions (this week)
1. **Put the price, size, material and delivery charge on every product post** (use the caption footer in `04`).
2. **Instant reply + saved replies + a one-message order form** (`07`). Target a reply in under 10 minutes, 10am–1am.
3. **Reshoot the top 5 bags** with the 7-shot standard on a plain background.
4. **Phone or WhatsApp confirmation before every dispatch.** Take an advance delivery charge for risky outside-Dhaka orders.
5. **Fill the Pricing tab for each SKU.** Know your break-even CAC before spending another taka on ads.

### 5. Three recommended hero products
1. **Everyday Laptop Tote (women):** working women, solves a real problem.
2. **Slim Briefcase (men):** office use plus a strong gifting angle.
3. **City Sling (unisex):** a lower-price entry product, very reel-friendly.

Final choice depends on your current best-sellers. If your order log shows a different winner, back the data.

### 6. Best content strategy
**Proof-led reels plus a transparent price.** Run 3–4 reels a week built on quality proof (stitch, hardware, load test), problem-solving (what fits) and trust (photo vs delivered, testimonials). Every post shows the price and has one CTA: Send Message. Judge content by **messages started per 1,000 reach**, not likes.

### 7. Best advertising strategy
Use **Messages campaigns** in Ads Manager on one or two hero products. Start broad in Dhaka + Chattogram + Sylhet, run 3 creatives, and test one variable per week. Add retargeting once warm audiences are big enough. Scale only when **cost per delivered order is below your target CAC**, and raise budgets 20–30% at a time.

### 8. Recommended monthly marketing budget
- Month 1: **~৳15–20k** (৳9k ads + content production).
- Month 2: **~৳30–33k**, only if Month 1 hits the CAC target.
- Month 3: **~৳55–60k**, only if Month 2 is profitable.

Profit decides the budget, not the calendar.

### 9. Expected operational improvements
Direction only. Actual numbers depend on your baseline.
- Faster replies and better scripts → a higher enquiry-to-order rate.
- Phone confirmation and advance delivery charges → fewer refused parcels, and lower courier losses.
- A visible price → fewer but better-quality enquiries, so less inbox time per sale.
- Weekly dashboard → you'll know which product, ad and area makes money.
