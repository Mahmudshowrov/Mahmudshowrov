# 4. 30-Day Sales Content Calendar

**Mix:** 12 Reels (R) · 8 Product showcases (P) · 4 Trust (T) · 3 Promotions (C) · 3 Brand story (S) = 30 posts.

**Posting time:** test 1pm and 9:30pm, since Bangladesh evening browsing usually peaks at night. Confirm with your own Insights after 2 weeks.

**Fixed caption footer (paste under every product post):**
```
💰 Price: ৳[price]
📏 Size: [L×H×W cm] | Material: [exact material]
🚚 Dhaka ৳[x] (1–2 days) · Outside Dhaka ৳[y] (2–4 days) · Cash on Delivery
🔁 7-day exchange · Open & check before payment
📩 Order: tap "Send Message" or WhatsApp [number]
```
Everything in [brackets] is a placeholder for you to fill. **Always show the real price.**

Audiences: **W** = women 22–40 (working, university, wedding guests), **M** = men 24–45 (office, business, gifting), **G** = gift buyers (both).

## Weekly routine that keeps this running

| Day | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday | Shoot day: all products for the week, 7 shots + 3 reels | 4 h |
| Sunday | Edit reels (CapCut), write captions | 3 h |
| Daily | Post + reply to comments within 30 min of posting | 30 min |
| Thursday | Review Insights: which posts brought **messages**, not just likes? | 30 min |

**Rule:** after 2 weeks, double down on the 3 formats that brought the most **messages started per 1,000 reach**, and drop the bottom 3.
