# 3. Premium Brand Strategy + Product & Pricing Strategy

## 3.1 Brand positioning

**Positioning line:** *Modern premium bags for people who notice details.*
**Proof line:** *Every stitch, zip and lining shown on camera before you buy.*

| Element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Personality | Calm, confident, minimal. Not loud "SALE!!! 🔥🔥". Think quiet premium. |
| Tone (Bangla/English) | Warm "apu/bhaiya" respect in chat. Short, clean English plus Bangla on posts. No exaggerated claims. |
| Colour palette | Base: off-white `#F4EFE8` and charcoal `#1F1F1F`. Accent: one only, such as deep cognac `#8A4B2A` or bottle green `#2F4A3A`. |
| Fonts | One serif for headlines (Playfair Display / Cormorant), one sans for details (Inter / Poppins). Use Hind Siliguri for Bangla. |
| Photo style | Solid neutral background, soft side light, 30–40% empty space, no clutter, no neon. |
| Logo | Wordmark "BAG LUX" with tight letter-spacing, plus a "BL" monogram for the profile picture, hardware stamp and dust bag. |
| Honesty rule | Label materials exactly: "Genuine cow leather", "Premium PU (vegan leather)", "Split leather". **Never call PU "leather".** In Bangladesh, the first refusal-on-delivery after a false claim ends the brand. |

> ⚠️ **Data gap:** I don't know BAG LUX's current materials or sourcing (local factory, Hazaribagh/Hemayatpur leather, China import, or a mix). The positioning works for both leather and good PU, but the **price ladder and claims change**. Confirm this first.

## 3.2 Quality & craftsmanship communication

Use the same mindset you apply to garment QC, because customers buy that confidence:

| Proof point | How to show it |
|---|---|
| Stitch density | Macro shot with a ruler: "8 stitches per inch, lock-stitched" |
| Edge finishing | Close-up of edge paint or turned edge |
| Hardware | Drop the buckle on a table so the weight is heard. Show the zip brand (YKK or another, if real). |
| Lining | Turn the bag inside out on camera |
| Strength | Load test: "5 kg of books for 24 hours, no sag" |
| Colourfastness / scratch | Wet tissue rub test and nail scratch test (only if it passes!) |
| Measurements | Tape measure on screen, plus a "fits iPhone Pro Max / 14" laptop / A4 file" shot |

## 3.3 Packaging & unboxing (budget-conscious)

| Item | Purpose | Rough approach |
|---|---|---|
| Printed or stamped kraft box (for hero products) | Unboxing reels, gift value | Use rubber-stamp logo on plain boxes at first. Print once volume justifies it. |
| Non-woven dust bag with logo | Feels premium and is reused by the customer, so it's free branding | Order in bulk from local bag makers |
| Tissue paper + sticker seal | Unboxing moment | Cheap, high impact |
| Thank-you / care card | Care tips, exchange policy, review QR code, referral code | Single card, printed both sides |
| Silica gel | Leather care, shows professionalism | Very cheap |

Track packaging as a **cost line per unit** in the costing sheet, exactly like trims and accessories in a garment cost breakdown.

## 3.4 Original collection direction (not copies)

**Design language — "Dhaka Modern":** clean architectural shapes, one signature detail repeated across all bags so people recognise a BAG LUX bag without a logo.

**Signature detail options (pick ONE and use it everywhere):**
1. A **contrast edge stripe** (e.g. cognac edge on a black bag).
2. A **geometric "BL" metal tab** on the side gusset.
3. A **signature lining colour**, for example bottle green inside every bag.

### Recommended hero products and why

1. **Everyday Laptop Tote (women):** it solves a real problem (office plus laptop plus style), it photographs well, and it has the widest audience among working women in Dhaka and Chattogram.

## 3.6 Photography standard: 7 shots per bag

1. Front, straight on, white background.
2. 45° angle.
3. Back + base (feet studs).
4. Inside open, showing lining and pockets.
5. Hardware and stitching macro.
6. Scale shot (on the body, or with an iPhone or laptop).
7. Lifestyle (office, café, rickshaw or Gulshan street, wedding setting).

Plus one 10-second "360 turn" video. This becomes your reusable asset bank for posts, ads and inbox replies.

## 3.7 Pricing & margin framework (fill with your real numbers)

This is the same build-up logic as an FOB cost sheet, but for a D2C parcel.

### Step 1 — Landed cost per unit (COGS)
```
Landed cost = Purchase / manufacturing price
            + Inbound freight & duty (if imported)
            + Packaging (box, dust bag, card, tissue, sticker)
            + Photography/sample cost ÷ expected units
            + QC rejects allowance (% of purchase price)
```

### Step 2 — Cost per delivered order (fulfilment)
```
Fulfilment = Courier charge (Dhaka / outside Dhaka mix)
           + COD charge (% of collected amount, e.g. Paperfly 1% outside same city)
           + Payment gateway fee (if bKash/card)
           + RTO cost allowance
```
**RTO (return-to-origin) allowance** is the hidden cost most Facebook sellers ignore:
```
RTO allowance per delivered order = (Return rate ÷ (1 − Return rate)) × (Forward courier + Return courier + Packaging damage)
```
Example (illustrative only): with a 15% return rate and ৳250 lost per returned parcel → 0.15 ÷ 0.85 × 250 ≈ ৳44 extra cost on **every** delivered order.

### Step 3 — Contribution margin before ads
```
Contribution before ads = Selling price − Delivery charge discount given − Landed cost − Fulfilment
```

### Step 4 — Maximum affordable customer acquisition cost (CAC)
```
Max CAC (break-even) = Contribution before ads
Target CAC           = Contribution before ads × (1 − desired profit share)
Target cost per enquiry (message) = Target CAC × Enquiry-to-delivered-order rate
```
Example (illustrative only, your numbers will differ): if contribution before ads is ৳1,200, you want to keep 50% as profit, and 1 in 8 enquiries becomes a delivered order:
- Target CAC = ৳600
- Target cost per message = ৳600 × 0.125 = **৳75**

That's how you set your **own** ad targets instead of copying "industry benchmarks".

### Step 5 — Pricing rules
- Price the hero products so **contribution before ads is at least 2× your target CAC**. That leaves room for testing.
- Use psychological endings (৳3,450 / ৳4,950) consistently.
- Don't run permanent "discounts". Use **bundle value** (free card holder) instead of cutting the price. A premium brand that is always on sale is not premium.
- Delivery: either charge it transparently or build it into the price and say "Free delivery all over BD". **Test both.** Free delivery often lifts conversion but needs the margin.
