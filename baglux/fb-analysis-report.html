Bottom line

  1. Your premium bags sell. Your cheap stock doesn't. Both KORRNE DIAO bags (around ৳4,000) sold. None of the 8 purses and wallets priced ৳610–1,035 has sold. The sample is small, but it backs the premium positioning.
  2. ৳28,400 of stock is sitting unsold, which is 61% of the ৳46,930 invested. Only 8 of 37 pieces have sold (22%). Chowk Bazar stock bought on 11 Aug is still mostly in hand.
  3. The page is hard to find by name. A search for "BAG LUX" returns at least 9 other bag pages with almost the same name (UK, Ukraine, Moldova and others). None of them is yours, and baglux.me doesn't show up at all.
  4. You can't measure the page yet. The Sales Tracker is empty, 23 products have no code, and 13 have no name. Without these, you can't tell which post, reel or ad brought an order.

03Pricing: can these margins pay for Facebook ads?

Your pricing rule is wholesale + 70% + ৳100 carry. That leaves this much per piece to cover ads, courier, COD charge and returns:

Buying priceSelling priceProfit before ads & deliveryCan it pay for ads?
৳550 purse৳1,035৳385Barely. One refused COD parcel wipes out the profit.
৳1,250 bag৳2,225৳875Possible if you keep cost per order low
৳2,400 bag৳4,180৳1,680Yes. This is where ad money should go.

How to set the limit: divide the profit per piece by 2. That's the most you should pay in ads per delivered order and still keep half the profit. For a KORRNE DIAO bag, that's about ৳840 per order. Track your real cost per order in the Sales Tracker before deciding.

  • Don't advertise the purses on their own. Sell them as add-ons ("matching purse ৳690 with any bag") or in bundles to clear the stock.
  • Fix row 25. It sold at ৳1,000 against ৳1,050 cost plus carry. Set a minimum price for every product: cost + carry + at least ৳300.
  • Put KORRNE DIAO-type bags in front: on the cover photo, in reels and in ads. The page's best sellers so far are at the premium end.
