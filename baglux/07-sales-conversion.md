# 7. Sales Conversion Playbook (Messenger / WhatsApp)

Set these up as **Saved Replies** in Meta Business Suite and as **Quick Replies** in WhatsApp Business. Replace the [brackets].

## 7.1 Service standards

| Standard | Target |
|---|---|
| First reply | Under 5 min (instant reply covers the gap) |
| Price | Always in the first reply. Never ask "konta?" without giving a price. |
| Coverage | 10am–1am (night is peak buying time) |
| Order confirmation | Within 10 min of getting the address |
| Dispatch | Same day if the order is confirmed by [cut-off time] |
| Tone | Respectful (apu/bhaiya), short, no pressure, no ALL CAPS |

## 7.2 Instant reply (auto)

**Bangla:**
> Assalamu alaikum 🤍 BAG LUX e shagotom! Kon bag ta apnar pochondo hoyeche, screenshot pathan. Amra 5 minute er moddhe price, size ar delivery details janacchi.
> 🚚 Cash on Delivery all over BD · 🔁 7-day exchange

**English:**
> Hi, welcome to BAG LUX 🤍 Send a screenshot of the bag you like and we'll share price, size and delivery details within 5 minutes. COD all over Bangladesh · 7-day exchange.

## 7.3 Product enquiry reply

**Bangla:**
> [Product name] apu/bhaiya 😊
> 💰 Price: ৳[price]
> 📏 Size: [L×H×W] — [ekta 14" laptop / A4 file / phone] easily dhore
> 🧵 Material: [exact material]
> 🎨 Colour: [colours] (stock e ache)
> 🚚 Dhaka ৳[x] (1–2 din) · Dhakar baire ৳[y] (2–4 din)
> Real video lagle bolun, ekhuni pathacchi. Order korte chaile nicher info ta din 👇

**English:**
> The [Product] is ৳[price]. Size [L×H×W], fits a [14" laptop]. Material: [exact]. Colours in stock: [colours]. Delivery: Dhaka ৳[x] (1–2 days), outside Dhaka ৳[y] (2–4 days). Want a real video? I'll send it right away.

## 7.5 Order confirmation

**Bangla:**
> Dhonnobad [Name] apu/bhaiya 🤍 Apnar order confirm:
> 🛍️ [Product] – [Colour] × [Qty]
> 💰 ৳[price] + delivery ৳[x] = **৳[total]**
> 📍 [Address]
> 🚚 Expected delivery: [date]
> Delivery man ashle parcel khule check kore tarpor payment korben. Kono problem hole 7 diner moddhe exchange 🤍

**English:**
> Thanks [Name], your order is confirmed: [Product, colour, qty]. Total ৳[total] including delivery. Expected by [date]. You can open and check the parcel before paying, and you have a 7-day exchange.

**Before dispatch, a short phone call or WhatsApp voice note:** *"Apu, BAG LUX theke bolchi, apnar [bag] ta aaj pathacchi, [date] e paben. Ei number e courier call dibe."* This single step usually reduces refusals more than anything else.

## 7.7 Abandoned enquiry recovery (3 touches)

Meta allows a promotional follow-up only within its messaging policy window (generally 24 hours after the customer's last message). After that, use approved message tags or utility templates only. Check the current Meta rules in Business Suite before automating.

| When | Bangla | English |
|---|---|---|
| +2 hours | "Apu, [Product] er kono prosno ache? Chaile real video pathate pari 🤍" | "Any questions about the [Product]? Happy to send a video." |
| +20 hours (within 24h) | "Apu, [colour] ta shudhu [2] ta baki ache (only if true). Order korte chaile shudhu nam, number, address din." | "Only [2] left in [colour]. Just send name, number and address to order." |
| WhatsApp (if they gave a number) +3 days | "BAG LUX theke bolchi 😊 Ei shoptaho [offer] cholche, apnar pochonder [Product] er jonno o projojjo." | "This week's offer applies to the [Product] you liked." |

Never fake scarcity. If stock isn't limited, use the offer deadline instead.

| Delivery +30 | "New collection eshese apu. Apnar jonno ৳[200] off code: [CODE]. Ei matching [card holder/pouch] ta apnar [bag] er sathe darun jay." |
| Eid / birthday (if known) | Gift-set reminder |

## 7.9 Referral program

- **Mechanic:** each customer gets a personal code (e.g. `RIYA200`). A friend gets ৳200 off, and the referrer gets ৳200 credit on their next order after the friend's order is **delivered** (not just ordered, so you aren't paying for refusals).
- **Track it** in the dashboard's "Customers" tab: code, referrals delivered, credit used.
- **Cost check:** the referral reward must be lower than your paid-ads CAC. If it is, every referral is cheaper than an ad.

## 7.11 RTO / fake-order protection

1. Phone or WhatsApp confirmation before dispatch.
2. Ask for an **advance delivery charge** via bKash/Nagad for outside Dhaka, high-value orders (above ৳[threshold]), and first-time numbers with incomplete addresses. Word it politely: *"Dhakar bairer order e shudhu delivery charge ta advance nicchi, bag er dam delivery te."*
3. Keep a **blacklist** of numbers that refused without reason.
4. Track the RTO % per area and per ad set in the dashboard. Cut areas where returns erase the profit.
